Frequently Asked Questions

How can I track my order delivery?
Once shipped, we email you a tracking ID. You can also view active shipment updates by opening the 'My Orders' section on this dashboard.
What is the return/refund policy?
We offer a 30-day return policy for all undamaged goods. Refund payments will be returned to your original payment card within 3-5 days.
What payments do you accept?
We support standard Debit/Credit cards, Net Banking, UPI/GPay/PhonePe transfer, and Cash on Delivery (COD).
Can I edit my shipping address?
Yes! Open 'My Profile' and update your Shipping Address. Saved details will automatically apply to all future orders.
